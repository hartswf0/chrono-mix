RETRO CACHE · SENSOR TRIALS

One engine, every stream the browser can reach. Each page keeps the last N seconds of a sensor in a pre-allocated ring buffer, persisting nothing — until you hit TRIGGER, and the past you already missed is recovered and piped ahead of the live stream. Grant each sensor's permission when the page asks; on phones, serve over HTTPS.
CAMERA
Rear camera at 24 fps, 4 s pre-roll. Scrub and replay the recovered frames. MICROPHONE
Live PCM, 10 s pre-roll. Play back and download the WAV of what was just said. IMU / MOTION
Accelerometer, gyroscope, orientation at ~60 Hz, 15 s pre-roll. CSV export. Synthetic mode for desktops. SENSOR LAB
Pointer/touch, geolocation, gyroscope, magnetometer, ambient light, battery — pick a feed, same engine. SYNTHETIC
The original canvas demo — no permissions needed anywhere.
